How We Rate

The standard every casino is measured against, and the rules that keep it honest.

The CFU Verified Standard

✦ ✦ ✦

A casino earns "CFU Verified" only by passing every step. Failing one means no badge, and no exceptions for paying partners.

Legal status checked

Real-money sites: we confirm the license in the regulator's public registry. Sweepstakes sites: we review the sweepstakes rules and check where the site is restricted or banned. Either way, each listing shows where it is legally available.

Terms read and quoted

We read the terms and quote the rules that matter most: withdrawals or redemptions, wagering requirements, limits and fees.

Cash-out tested

We complete a real withdrawal or redemption ourselves and record the date, method and how long it took.

Complaint history reviewed

We look for patterns in public complaints and document what we find and where.

Player protections present

Age checks, limit-setting tools, and clear responsible-gambling information.

Re-checked on a schedule

Every listing shows its "last verified" date. If a casino stops passing, it comes off the list.

One standard for every kind of casino

✦ ✦ ✦

Real-money and sweepstakes

Both are judged by the same steps and get the same treatment. Neither type is ranked above the other just for being that type.

Cash, crypto or other payouts

How a casino pays out (cash, crypto, gift cards) is shown as a label. It is not a penalty or a bonus. What counts is whether it actually pays.

Where you live matters

Rules differ by U.S. state and Canadian province. We show where each casino is available, and we don't show or promote a casino to people in places where it isn't allowed.

Labels you'll see

✦ ✦ ✦

Verified

Checked by us, evidence shown, date shown.

Unverified report

Reported to us, not confirmed. We say so.

Couldn't verify

We tried and couldn't confirm it. We never fill the gap with a guess.

Our independence rules

✦ ✦ ✦

  • Ratings come from the checklist above, never from whether a casino pays us.
  • If a casino fails, it isn't listed, even if it offers us an affiliate deal.
  • Every page with a paid link says so. See the Affiliate Disclosure.
  • We don't publish a numeric score until we've published exactly how it's calculated.
  • Wrong about something? Tell us and we'll correct it and note the change.
