Support & Responsible Gambling

If gambling stops being fun, free help is available any time.

National Problem Gambling Helpline (U.S.)

Free, confidential, 24/7.

  • Call 1-800-522-4700
  • Text 800GAM
  • Chat online at ncpgambling.org/chat

Run by the National Council on Problem Gambling. The old 1-800-GAMBLER number is no longer used for this service.

Help in Canada

Free and confidential. Help lines by province:

  • Ontario (ConnexOntario): 1-866-531-2600
  • British Columbia: 1-888-795-6111
  • Alberta: 1-866-332-2322
  • Saskatchewan: 1-800-306-6789
  • Manitoba: 1-800-463-1554

Other provinces and territories: see the Responsible Gambling Council's help for Canadians page.

Simple habits that help

✦ ✦ ✦

Set limits first

Decide your time and money limit before you start, and stick to it.

Only play with spare money

Never gamble with money needed for rent, bills or food.

Don't chase losses

If you're losing and feel the urge to win it back, that's the moment to stop.

Take breaks

Step away regularly. If you can't, that's a signal worth paying attention to.

Use the site's tools

Most reputable sites offer deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion. Use them.
